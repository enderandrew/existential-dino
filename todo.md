## Features

* Swipe left slow / Swipe right dash
* In-run micro-challenges
* Descending Sky Debris: Display a flashing vertical beacon on the ground 1.2 seconds before an errant meteor or fossil shard impacts, forcing a rapid duck or stutter-step.
* The Leap of Faith (Kierkegaard's Chasm): An impossibly wide pit spawns that cannot be cleared by jump arc alone. Surviving requires jumping directly toward the center and trusting an invisible platform to render underfoot at the final frame before impact.
* Lucidity streaks — reward near-misses (grazing an obstacle without hitting it) rather than just distance survived. Camus's absurd hero doesn't look away from the wall — he stares it down and keeps walking. A running "lucidity" counter that boosts score the closer you cut it, rather than the safer you play, rewards confrontation over avoidance.
* Solipsism Void: At a game over, the right third of the canvas fades from rendered terrain into boxes showing the collision, with labels and raw hexadecimal background codes.
* Nietzschean Déjà Vu: A stretch of the same obstacle 5 times with a spoken comment on Déjà Vu.
* The Illusion of Choice (Forking Paths): The track splits into an elevated plateau (two platform jumps up?) and a ground trench, giving the visual impression of agency, but both paths converge on the exact same hazard configuration, accompanied by a dry spoken observation on determinism.
* Echo runs — on death, have a faint, semi-transparent "ghost" of your best-or-most-recent obstacle pattern replay in the background of your next attempt, unreachable and non-interactive — you, watching yourself, forever slightly out of phase. This is the most technically involved of the list (you'd need to record obstacle-spawn timing and speed to replay it) but it's the single idea most literally "eternal recurrence."
* Schrödinger's Cacti (The Observer Effect): Translucent outline obstacles that only collapse into solid hitboxes when an input key is active. Tapping Jump or Duck solidifies them into lethal spikes, while running past without pressing anything lets the sprite phase through immaterial wireframes.
* The Ship of Theseus: Rather than a sudden total swap during an Identity Crisis, individual sprite components glitch and swap piecemeal every 300 meters—a Batman cowl, a Mario running leg, a Covid virus torso. Eventually, not a single original pixel of the starting silhouette remains.
* Down-Drop Shockwave: Slamming downward via the fast-fall drop (holding Down in mid-air) emits a short horizontal shockwave upon impact, shattering any small single cactus within a 30-pixel radius.
* Ledge Wall-Kick: Allow players to tap Jump against the vertical face of an elevated platform or tall obstacle to perform a reactive rebound leap rather than suffering a face-plant collision.
* Absurd Momentum: Multiplies score accumulation by 3x and grants invulnerability for 5 seconds, but completely disables the jump button, leaving the player entirely at the mercy of horizontal speed.
* Memento Mori: Freezes all obstacle movement and mutes audio to a hollow low-pass heartbeat for 4 seconds, after which all frozen obstacles snap forward at 1.25x speed.
